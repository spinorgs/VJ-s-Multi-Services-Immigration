Live Scan Fingerprinting

Electronic fingerprinting for authorized California DOJ and FBI background-check requests.

Practical support for your next step.

For employment, licensing, volunteering, and other authorized requests, start with the instructions from your requesting agency.

How we can help

  • Bring your completed Request for Live Scan Service form and acceptable current identification.
  • Follow your requesting agency’s instructions for the required submission and level of service.
  • Call before visiting to confirm availability, requirements, and charges.

The fingerprint rolling fee and applicable government processing fees are explained before service. Reviewing agencies control processing times, results, and eligibility decisions. No agency endorsement or approval is implied.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Start with your requesting agency

  • Confirm which background check and level of service your agency requires.
  • Bring its completed Live Scan request form and acceptable current identification.

What to expect

Government processing fees are separate from the rolling fee. Reviewing agencies control processing and results.
