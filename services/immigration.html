What we help with

Immigration paperwork support without confusion.

  • Family-based and employment-based immigration
  • Green cards, citizenship, naturalization, and visa applications
  • USCIS forms, document preparation, and adjustment of status
  • Consular processing and document translation coordination
  • Document evidence checklists and appointment preparation
  • Attorney referral and consultation coordination when needed
How it works

A practical checklist-driven process.

We review the goal, organize required records, identify missing items, prepare eligible documents, and help clients understand the next appointment or filing step.

VJ's Immigration Consultant is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Legal advice and representation are coordinated only through independently licensed attorneys.
