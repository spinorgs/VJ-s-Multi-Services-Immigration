Notary Services

Personal and business notarial services from a commissioned California notary public.

Practical support for your next step.

A little preparation helps your visit go smoothly. Discuss the requested notarial act and document requirements before coming in.

How we can help

  • Call to confirm notary availability, identification requirements, and fees.
  • Bring the document and acceptable identification; personal appearance requirements apply.
  • Wait to sign until instructed by the notary.

A notary performs the requested notarial act; notarization does not establish the truth or legal validity of document contents. The notary cannot choose legal documents or provide legal advice.
