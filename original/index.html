Questions

Straight answers before you book.

Is VJ's Immigration Consultant a law firm?

No. VJ's Immigration Consultant is an immigration consulting business and is not a law firm. Legal advice and representation are provided only by independently licensed attorneys.

Can you help with immigration forms?

Yes. We help with immigration document preparation, USCIS forms, green cards, citizenship, visa applications, adjustment of status, consular processing, and related coordination.

Do you offer DOJ & FBI Live Scan fingerprinting?

Yes. DOJ & FBI Live Scan fingerprinting is a main service for background checks, employment, licensing, and immigration-related requirements.

Do you support tax services for clients in different locations?

Yes. We support individual and business tax needs, including ITIN applications and renewals, tax planning, IRS letter assistance, and bookkeeping support.
