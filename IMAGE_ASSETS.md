# Service imagery

Two new illustrative assets were generated with the built-in image_gen tool, then converted to optimized JPEGs (1100px longest edge, quality 80). Originals remain in the generation library. No CLI generation or API keys were used.

Saved project files:
- `assets/fingerprint-desk.jpg`
- `assets/notary-desk.jpg`

These are illustrative equipment/document still lifes, not photographs of VJ’s equipment, premises or staff. The website labels service imagery accordingly. Existing local reference photography supports administrative, immigration resource, legal resource and tax referral content. Photographs now display in their original colors without grayscale, multiply blending, or tint overlays on service cards. Caption contrast overlays remain where needed.

## Final prompts

### Fingerprint scanner
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: illustrative editorial website photograph, wide 3:2 landscape. Premium close-up still life of a generic unbranded desktop electronic fingerprint capture scanner with a dark glass platen, positioned diagonally on a sand-colored desk, beside a blank appointment checklist and a simple pen. Espresso brown #352820 and warm sand #E9D5BB monochromatic palette, natural side lighting, tactile matte surfaces, refined documentary product photography, shallow depth of field. Device is illustrative, not a specific brand or government-issued model. No people, no fingerprints or biometric data displayed, no government seals, no logos, no screen text, no readable document text, no watermarks. Compose entire useful device in central two thirds for responsive cropping. Realistic and understated, not futuristic.

### Document and notary desk
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: illustrative editorial website photograph, wide 3:2 landscape. Carefully composed overhead still life of a closed unbranded notarial embosser and a simple rubber stamp, stacked blank sand-colored papers clipped neatly together, a fountain pen and a tabbed document folder, on a warm textured sand desk. Sophisticated Espresso #352820 and Sand #E9D5BB monochromatic palette, soft window light and tactile paper textures, editorial product photography. Practical document preparation theme. No humans, no signature, no visible stamp impression, no government seals, no badges, no logos, no readable text, no fake credentials. Central objects with breathing room for responsive crops. Authentic objects and restrained contemporary styling.

Service image filenames, alt text, preparation guidance and expectations are centralized in site-config.js. Available-service images alone are copied to dist; bail bonds receive no imagery or intake action.
