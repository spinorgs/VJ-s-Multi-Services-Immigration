BEFORE YOU VISIT

Good questions.
Straightforward answers.

A few things to know before getting started.

All frequently asked questions
Do I need an appointment?+

Please call to confirm availability and requirements before visiting. A website inquiry does not confirm an appointment.

What should I bring for Live Scan?+

Bring your completed Request for Live Scan Service form, acceptable current identification, and instructions from your requesting agency.

Do you provide legal or immigration advice?+

No. We provide administrative assistance and public professional resources. We do not assess eligibility, choose legal forms, prepare immigration forms, or provide legal advice or representation.

Do you prepare tax returns?+

Our tax service is referral only. Your independent tax professional handles advice, preparation, filing, and fees under a separate agreement.
