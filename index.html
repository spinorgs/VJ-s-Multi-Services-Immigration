05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

GOOD QUESTIONS.
STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Still have a question?
Let’s talk it through

What immigration support do you provide?+

We help with document preparation, USCIS forms, green cards, citizenship, visa applications, adjustment of status, consular processing, and related coordination.

Is VJ’s a law firm?+

No. VJ’s Immigration Consultant is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Legal advice and representation are provided only by independently licensed attorneys. A $250 upfront consultation charge applies to legal consultation coordination.

What should I bring for Live Scan?+

Bring valid identification and your agency’s request form. Request a preferred date and time below so the team can confirm availability and preparation details.

Can you help with taxes and ITINs?+

Yes. Services include individual and business tax preparation, ITIN applications and renewals, tax planning, IRS letter assistance, and bookkeeping support.

How do I arrange an appointment?+

Prepare your consultation request below, including your service and preferred appointment time. Availability must be confirmed by the team.
