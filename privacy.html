Information on this website

This website uses no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, embedded maps, or third-party fonts. When form sending is configured, Cloudflare Turnstile performs security checks to help prevent spam. Ordinary hosting may record technical information such as IP address, request time, browser details, and requested page. The hosting provider’s logging and retention settings must be confirmed before publication.

Contact inquiries

The appointment form collects your name, email, phone, preferred date, selected service, general message, and privacy consent so the business can respond. When configured, a Firebase Cloud Function sends these details through Resend to the business inbox. Replies are addressed to the email you supplied. Submission does not confirm an appointment. Cloudflare Turnstile processes security information when you interact with the configured form. If configuration is incomplete, online sending remains unavailable. Do not send sensitive documents through this site.

External services

Opening directions takes you to Google Maps. Calling uses your device’s calling application. Those providers apply their own privacy practices.

Your choices and questions

Call the business to ask about information you have shared or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations. No customer account or form storage in your browser is used. The backend stores keyed hashes and limited delivery/rate-limit metadata in Firestore for duplicate prevention and abuse controls, with a 48-hour expiry policy. Submitted message bodies, names, emails, phone numbers and raw IP addresses are not stored in those records. TTL deletion is asynchronous. Resend and the business inbox process the submitted details for email delivery and response; their retention must be approved by the business. Turnstile may process device/network signals under Cloudflare’s privacy practices. It does not respond differently to Do Not Track signals because it does not track visitors across websites.

Before publication

The business must approve mailbox/provider retention periods, the privacy contact, and Firebase, Resend, and Cloudflare practices before publication. Changes to data collection require an updated policy.
