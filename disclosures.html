Legal & Service Disclosures

Know the scope of your support.

Please review this information before contacting us.

Draft for owner review.

Administrative assistance

General administrative help is limited to organization and routine correspondence from your instructions. It does not include legal, immigration, or tax advice.

Tax referrals

Tax referrals connect customers with independent tax professionals. VJ’s does not advertise direct tax preparation, tax planning, or IRS representation through this service.

Immigration and legal resources

We provide access to public directories and certified referral resources. Customers choose their professionals. We do not provide immigration consultation, prepare immigration forms, assess eligibility, select legal forms or recommend strategies. Appointment and record organization support follows your instructions or the professional’s checklist. We do not operate as a certified lawyer referral service or provide attorney matching.

Live Scan and notary

Requesting agencies determine the required fingerprint submission. Government processing charges are separate from rolling fees; agencies control results and timing. A notary performs notarial acts subject to California requirements and cannot select legal documents or give legal advice. Notarization does not confirm the truth or legal validity of document contents.

Professional advice

This business is not presented as a law firm. Seek legal advice from a licensed attorney or, for immigration matters, an appropriately authorized representative.

Availability

Only services shown as available can be selected for an inquiry. Bail bonds are coming soon and are not available for applications, bookings, or payments. No government endorsement, result, or processing time is guaranteed.

Fees and appointments

Confirm any service fee and appointment directly with the provider. No legal consultation or referral fee is collected through this website.

Important service notice

VJ’s Multi-Services Center is a private business, unaffiliated with USCIS, California DOJ, FBI, IRS, or other government agencies. We are not a law firm and provide no legal advice or representation. Administrative assistance does not include choosing legal forms or recommending legal strategies. Independent professionals set their own services and fees under separate agreements. Government approvals, processing times, and legal or tax outcomes are not guaranteed.
