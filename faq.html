COMMON QUESTIONS

A little more clarity.

Useful information before you get in touch.

Are you a law firm?

No. We provide administrative assistance and professional resources, without legal advice or representation.

Can you choose immigration forms for me?

No. Eligibility, form selection, legal deadlines, and strategy must be discussed with an authorized immigration legal professional.

What should I bring for Live Scan?

Bring your completed Request for Live Scan Service form, acceptable current identification, and your requesting agency’s instructions. Call first to confirm requirements.

How much do services cost?

Call for current pricing. Our charges are explained before service. Government processing and independent professional fees are separate.

Are bail bonds available?

No. Bail bond services are inactive. Any future launch depends on required licensing and approvals.

Do I need an appointment?

Call to confirm availability before visiting. Website inquiries do not confirm appointments.

Do you prepare tax returns?

The advertised tax service is referral only. Independent tax professionals arrange their own engagements.

Can I send documents through the form?

Please do not send sensitive records. Ask the provider for an appropriate secure channel.

Important service notice

VJ’s Multi-Services Center is a private business, unaffiliated with USCIS, California DOJ, FBI, IRS, or other government agencies. We are not a law firm and provide no legal advice or representation. Administrative assistance does not include choosing legal forms or recommending legal strategies. Independent professionals set their own services and fees under separate agreements. Government approvals, processing times, and legal or tax outcomes are not guaranteed.
